## Executive Summary

Evert Calderon's Mesa Group Consulting operates a **dual-affiliate strategy** leveraging two distinct financial technology platforms. **SuperMoney** serves as his partner for mainstream lending products (credit cards, personal loans, auto/student loan refinancing, debt consolidation), while **Novae** — an Inc. 5000 fintech focused on underserved communities — serves as his partner for specialized services (debt relief, life insurance, trust & will plans, business debt relief, business credit). Together, these two affiliate relationships let Evert monetize virtually the entire financial services journey of his audience.

---

## Detailed Findings

### 1. SuperMoney: The Personal Finance Platform

SuperMoney, trusted by over 2 million members since 2013, is a personal finance platform that provides a comprehensive suite of tools for budgeting, tracking net worth, improving credit scores, and managing finances [1]. Its platform covers financial product categories including credit cards, personal loans, student loan refinancing, auto loan refinancing, and debt consolidation — the same categories listed on Evert's Miro board as "supermoney affiliate" pages.

SuperMoney uses proprietary "Sense AI" technology to analyze users' finances [1]. Based on the Miro board labels, Evert appears to participate in SuperMoney's affiliate or partner program: he builds dedicated content pages on his own website for each product vertical (credit cards, personal loans, etc.) that drive traffic to SuperMoney, where visitors can compare and apply for financial products. Evert likely earns commissions when referred visitors complete applications or fund loans through the platform.

### 2. Novae: A Diversified Fintech Serving Underserved Communities

Novae, LLC is a Georgia-based (Conyers, GA) fintech company founded in 2014 that made the Inc. 5000 list of America's Fastest-Growing Private Companies for five consecutive years (2021–2025) [2]. The company describes its mission as providing "greater access to credit, capital and entrepreneurship for underserved communities nationwide" [2].

Key details about Novae:

- **Debt Relief / Restructuring:** Novae maintains "exclusive partnerships with licensed debt restructuring firms for consumers and small businesses, reducing their debts and payments up to 90% in some cases" [2].
- **Business Credit Building:** Forbes reported that "Novae is helping business owners establish business credit scores then leveraging the scores to access cash and credit" [3].
- **Personal and Business Loans:** Novae offers personal loans (including debt consolidation) and small business loan programs through a lender network [4].
- **Lending Technology:** Novae Financing operates a "patent-pending proprietary technology" that powers a "multi-lender point of sale platform" for in-store, online, and mobile lending [3].
- **Additional Products:** The Novae website and affiliated pages indicate offerings in life insurance, trust and estate planning, and business debt relief — the same categories Evert labels as "novae" on his Miro board.

Novae's website indicates it has received media attention from major outlets including Forbes, Yahoo Finance, Black Enterprise, CNBC, and others [3].

**How Evert uses it:** The Miro board labels five pages as "novae" — debt relief, trust & will plan, life insurance, business debt relief, and business credit. These are services that typically require specialized providers (licensed debt negotiators, insurance carriers, estate planning professionals, business credit consultants) rather than simple comparison shopping. Evert likely operates as a referral partner or affiliate for Novae, directing his audience to Novae's service offerings and earning commissions when leads convert into enrolled clients.

### 3. The Complementary Strategy

Evert's Miro board reveals a deliberately segmented affiliate approach:

| **Product Category** | **Partner** | **Model** |
|---|---|---|
| Credit Cards | SuperMoney | Affiliate pages driving to SuperMoney platform |
| Personal Loans | SuperMoney | Affiliate pages driving to SuperMoney platform |
| Auto Loan Refinancing | SuperMoney | Affiliate pages driving to SuperMoney platform |
| Student Loan Refi | SuperMoney | Affiliate pages driving to SuperMoney platform |
| Debt Consolidation Loans | SuperMoney | Affiliate pages driving to SuperMoney platform |
| Debt Relief | Novae | Referral to Novae's debt restructuring partners |
| Trust & Will Plans | Novae | Referral to Novae's estate planning services |
| Life Insurance | Novae | Referral to Novae's insurance products |
| Business Debt Relief | Novae | Referral to Novae's business debt restructuring |
| Business Credit | Novae | Referral to Novae's business credit building program |

**The logic is clear:** SuperMoney provides a platform for **financial product access** — users can explore and manage credit cards, loans, and refinancing options. Novae provides **specialized financial services** — debt restructuring, credit building, insurance, and estate planning that involve negotiation, counseling, or ongoing professional relationships. These aren't products you simply "shop for" — they're services you enroll in.

By combining both, Evert can capture his audience at multiple touchpoints in their financial journey:

- A visitor who wants a better credit card → SuperMoney affiliate page → commission
- A visitor overwhelmed with debt → Novae debt relief referral → commission
- A small business owner needing capital → Novae business credit referral → commission
- Someone with student loans → SuperMoney refinancing page → commission

This is a comprehensive **financial services affiliate funnel** that monetizes the full spectrum of consumer and small business financial needs.

## Uncertainty & Gaps

- **SuperMoney's specific affiliate program details** (commission rates, cookie duration, signup process) were not publicly available on their website during research. SuperMoney may operate its affiliate program through private partnerships or networks rather than a public-facing publisher portal. The characterization of SuperMoney as a comparison marketplace is inferred from its product categories and the Miro board's "affiliate" labeling, not from an explicit program description on SuperMoney's site.
- **Novae's affiliate/referral program structure** (compensation model, onboarding process) was not detailed on their public-facing pages. It's possible Novae operates through direct business relationships rather than a self-serve affiliate platform.
- **Mesa Group Consulting's website** was not directly reviewed, so the exact implementation of these affiliate pages (widgets, links, co-branded experiences) could not be confirmed.
- The **"trust & will plan" and "life insurance" products** under Novae are not extensively detailed on Novae's public website. These may be offered through sub-partners within Novae's network.
- **Novae's coverage of all 50 states and Puerto Rico** was mentioned on the Novae Money loans page [4] but not captured in the primary homepage excerpts [2].
- No direct connection between Evert Calderon/Mesa Group Consulting and these companies was found in public records — the analysis is based entirely on the Miro board labels matching the companies' product verticals.
