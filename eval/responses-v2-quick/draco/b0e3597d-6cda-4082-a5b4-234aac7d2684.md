# Heat Pump Dryer Comparison Report: Melbourne Heritage Terrace

## Models Compared
- **Bosch Serie 8** (WTX88M20AU) — 8kg heat pump dryer
- **LG Series 9** (DVH9-09W) — 9kg heat pump dryer

## Executive Summary

For a Melbourne heritage terrace with limited ventilation running 6–8 weekly loads, the **LG DVH9-09W** presents the strongest case based on confirmed data, combining a confirmed 9-star Australian energy rating, a self-cleaning condenser critical for low-maintenance high-frequency use, ventless installation suited to heritage constraints, and the longest compressor warranty (10 years parts). Data gaps — particularly for the Bosch WTX88M20AU and for published AS/NZS 2442.1 standardised test results — limit direct performance benchmarking across all three models.

## 5. Installation & Heritage Overlay Considerations

### Ventilation (Critical for Heritage Terraces)

The LG DVH9-09W is confirmed as a "Heat Pump Condenser Dryer (Ventless)" [1], and Samsung's DV90T6240LH is likewise a heat pump condenser model that does not require external venting. Heat pump dryers as a category are ventless — they collect moisture internally rather than exhausting humid air. This is essential for heritage-listed terraces where:
- External wall penetrations require heritage permits
- Facade alterations are typically prohibited
- Limited subfloor/roof space restricts ducting runs

### Physical Dimensions

| Model | Width × Height × Depth | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **LG DVH9-09W** | **600 × 850 × 690 mm** | **58 kg** [1] |
| **Samsung DV90T6240LH** | Standard 600mm width (exact dimensions not confirmed) | ~55 kg (est.) |
| **Bosch WTX88M20AU** | Standard 600mm width (exact dimensions not confirmed) | ~50 kg (est.) |

All fit standard 600mm undercounter spaces common in Melbourne terrace renovations. The LG features a **reversible door** for flexible installation in tight spaces [1].

### Heritage Permit Requirements
- Internal appliance installation (non-structural, no facade impact) typically does not require a heritage permit
- If plumbing/drainage modifications are needed for condensate disposal, a building permit may be required (check with local council)
- Standard 10A power point is sufficient for all models

## 6. Condenser Cleaning & Maintenance

| Model | Condenser Maintenance | Filter System |
|---|---|---|
| **LG DVH9-09W** | **Auto Cleaning Condenser** — "The dryer automatically cleans the condenser during operation" [1] | Standard lint filter |
| **Samsung DV90T6240LH** | Manual cleaning; includes 2-in-1 filter system [2] | 2-in-1 consolidated filter |
| **Bosch WTX88M20AU** | Manual condenser cleaning required (typical for Bosch dryers) | Standard filter |

**This is a significant differentiator.** The LG's automatic condenser cleaning is confirmed: "Maintenance is simple thanks to the Auto Cleaning Condenser system. The dryer automatically cleans the condenser during operation" [1]. At 6–8 loads per week (350–416 loads/year), manual condenser cleaning on other brands is typically recommended every 2–4 weeks. The LG eliminates this task entirely.

For the Samsung and Bosch, the condenser unit must be manually removed, rinsed, and dried — a 10–15 minute task. Neglecting this maintenance leads to progressively longer drying times and higher energy consumption.

### Parts Availability in Australia

- **LG:** Strong Australian presence with a national service network
- **Samsung:** Well-supported through Samsung service centres in Australia
- **Bosch:** Parts available through BSH Home Appliances (Australian distributor)

All three brands have authorised service agents in metropolitan Melbourne.

## 8. Recommendation

### For this specific scenario — Melbourne heritage terrace, 6–8 loads/week, limited ventilation:

**First choice: LG DVH9-09W**
- Confirmed 9-star energy rating [1] supports lowest estimated running costs
- Auto-cleaning condenser eliminates the most tedious maintenance task at high usage volumes [1]
- 10-year compressor warranty (parts) provides long-term cost protection [1]
- Ventless operation and reversible door suit constrained heritage spaces [1]
- CHOICE 2025 Best Dryer Brand designation [1]

**Second choice: Samsung DV90T6240LH**
- Confirmed 194 kWh annual consumption is competitive [2]
- 2-in-1 filter simplifies maintenance
- 63 dB noise is the only confirmed figure in this comparison [2]
- Generally priced competitively at retail

**Third choice: Bosch WTX88M20AU**
- The 8kg capacity (vs. 9kg for the other two) means marginally more loads per week for the same laundry volume
- This specific model has limited current specification data available online, making direct comparison difficult
- Manual condenser cleaning adds ongoing maintenance burden at 6–8 loads/week
- Bosch's strong brand reputation in Australia is noted, but cannot be quantified against the other two from sources consulted
