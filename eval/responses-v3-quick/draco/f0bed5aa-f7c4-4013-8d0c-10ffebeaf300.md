## Analysis

### What Is Available vs. What Is Missing

**Q1 2024 data** was successfully extracted from CME Group's press release and secondary coverage. Key findings:

- **Operating Cash Flow Conversion (Q1 2024):** With GAAP net income of $855.2 million and cash/equivalents declining from $2.91 billion to $1.44 billion, working capital was a significant drag. The $92.6 million increase in accounts receivable indicates strong revenue growth (record ADV across multiple product lines) outpacing collections — a sign of positive business momentum but temporarily depressing cash conversion. Operating margin was an exceptional 64% ($960M / $1.5B).

- **Capital Allocation (Q1 2024):** CME returned $2.3 billion in dividends in a single quarter, vastly exceeding its $855 million in net income. This demonstrates an aggressive variable-dividend policy funded by accumulated cash reserves and operational cash generation. Cumulative variable dividends since 2012 reached $24.8 billion.

- **Debt Profile (Q1 2024):** Total debt of $3.4 billion against ~$1.7 billion in cash yields a net debt of ~$1.7 billion. The debt was split approximately 22% short-term ($749.3M) and 78% long-term ($2.68B).

**Q1 2025 data could not be extracted.** The 10-Q filing (filed April 30, 2025) and related press release were identified but not machine-readable through the available browsing tools. Similarly, the detailed fixed-rate notes maturity schedule spanning 2028–2048 was referenced in the Q1 2024 data and in new 424B5 prospectus filings (March 3 and March 4, 2025 for new debt issuances) but the specific note-by-note breakdown (principal, rate, maturity) could not be extracted.
