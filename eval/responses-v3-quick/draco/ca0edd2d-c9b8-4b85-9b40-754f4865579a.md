## Executive Summary

Evert Calderon's Mesa Group Consulting operates a **dual-platform affiliate marketing model**. He uses **SuperMoney** as a comparison-shopping affiliate for standardized lending products (credit cards, personal loans, auto/student loan refinancing, debt consolidation) where he earns per-lead commissions. For specialized financial services (debt relief, life insurance, estate planning, business credit, business debt relief), he partners with **Novae Money**, a multi-level marketing fintech company that aggregates third-party financial services and pays referral commissions to its promoters.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. SuperMoney: The "KAYAK of Financial Products"

**What it is:** SuperMoney (supermoney.com) is a financial technology platform and comparison engine founded in 2013, now trusted by 2 million members. SuperMoney's own affiliate listing describes it as providing "a KAYAK-like comparison shopping experience" for financial products [2].

**Products offered:** Credit cards, personal loans, auto loan refinancing, student loan refinancing, and debt consolidation loans [1][2]. The homepage also references a feature called "Sense AI" that "analyzes your finances without accessing personally identifiable information," as well as a consolidated view of "Assets, liabilities, and cash flow. All in one view" [1].

**Affiliate model:** SuperMoney's affiliate program (distributed through networks like FlexOffers) operates on a **pay-per-lead basis** — affiliates "earn commissions per qualified lead instead of waiting for a funded loan" [2]. The platform boasts "high conversion rates" because "most leading lenders are integrated with SuperMoney meaning more approvals than any single lender," resulting in "some of the highest EPCs in the space" [2].

**How Evert uses it:** On his Miro board, Evert marks five new pages as "SuperMoney affiliate":
| Page | Product Type |
|------|-------------|
| Credit Cards | Comparison/recommendation |
| Personal Loans | Comparison engine |
| Auto Loan Refinance | Rate comparison |
| Student Loan Refi | Rate comparison |
| Debt Consolidation Loan | Comparison engine |

Each of these pages on the Mesa Group website would presumably drive traffic to SuperMoney's comparison tools. When a visitor clicks through and generates a qualifying lead, Evert earns a commission. This is a low-risk, low-overhead monetization strategy — he doesn't need to originate loans or manage lender relationships; SuperMoney handles all of that.

### 2. Novae Money: MLM Commission Aggregator for Specialized Financial Services

**What it is:** Novae (Novae Money) is a Georgia-based MLM/fintech company founded by Reco McDaniel McCambry. It does **not directly provide** financial services. Instead, it operates as a "commission aggregator" — "they funnel users into other platforms — and promoters get paid for the referral" [4].

**Products and pricing:**
- **Business Credit Building** — Described as a way to "separate your business from personal credit with a proven, step‑by‑step system," retailing at "$995 for a DIY Suite or $2495 for a Guided Suite" [3]
- **Debt Relief** (consumer and business) — Third-party referral
- **Life Insurance** — Third-party referral
- **Trust & Will / Estate Planning** — Third-party referral
- Credit monitoring, business funding, and other financial services [3][4]

**MLM structure and costs:**
- "To become a Novae Money promoter, you pay: $349 upfront, $99 per month ongoing" [4]
- BehindMLM characterizes the company as operating on a complex MLM compensation structure and has raised concerns about pyramid-scheme dynamics [3]
- "Novae Money itself admits the average participant earned around $1,400 in a year" [4]; BehindMLM similarly reports that "the average participant in this business earned $1408 in 2023" [3]

**Controversies:** BehindMLM flagged potential pyramid scheme dynamics and reported that "a search of the SEC's Edgar database reveals neither Novae Money or McCambry/McDaniel is registered with the SEC. This makes Novae Money's offer of shares not only virtual but also illegal" [3].

**How Evert uses it:** On his Miro board, Evert marks five new pages as "Novae":
| Page | Product Type |
|------|-------------|
| Debt Relief | Consumer debt negotiation/settlement referral |
| Trust & Will Plan | Estate planning referral |
| Life Insurance | Insurance product referral |
| Business Debt Relief | Business debt negotiation referral |
| Business Credit | Credit-building system ($995–$2,495) |

These are all product categories that **SuperMoney doesn't cover** — they're specialized services, not comparison-shopping loan products. Novae fills that gap in Evert's product suite, allowing Mesa Group Consulting to offer a broader menu of financial services to its clients.

### 3. The Overall Strategy: How Mesa Group Consulting Leverages Both Platforms

Evert Calderon has constructed a **full-spectrum financial services affiliate funnel**:

1. **Traffic acquisition:** Mesa Group Consulting's website (and presumably its credit repair/credit monitoring clients) serves as the traffic source. People coming for credit help are naturally interested in better loan rates, debt relief, and financial planning.

2. **Product segmentation by partner:**
   - **SuperMoney** handles the **commoditized lending products** — things where comparison shopping makes sense (loans, credit cards, refinancing). The pay-per-lead model means Evert gets paid even if the consumer doesn't ultimately fund a loan.
   - **Novae** handles the **specialized/advisory products** — debt relief, estate planning, insurance, and business credit building. These are higher-ticket items where Novae's third-party referral model generates commissions.

3. **Revenue model:** Evert earns commissions from two distinct streams — lead-generation fees from SuperMoney and referral commissions from Novae — while his own website serves as the storefront that organizes and funnels traffic to both.
