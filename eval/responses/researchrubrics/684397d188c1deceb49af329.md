# San Francisco Summer Apartment Guide for Students

## Executive Summary

Finding a 2-bedroom, cat-friendly apartment in central San Francisco with parking and gym access for under $2,200/month (your individual share) is achievable but tight. SF's average rent is $3,724/month, and short-term summer leases command a premium. With 1–2 roommates splitting costs, your best bets are neighborhoods like **SoMa, Civic Center, Inner Sunset**, and potentially the **Marina**. Below is a comprehensive guide covering specific apartment listings, neighborhood safety, budget realities, roommate resources, and practical advice.

## 4. Cat-Friendly Considerations

Most listings reviewed do not explicitly advertise pet policies on listing sites. You **must** contact each property directly to confirm:
- ✅ Cats are allowed
- The **pet deposit** amount (typically $200–$500 one-time)
- The **monthly pet rent** (typically $50–$100/month)
- Any **breed/weight restrictions** (less common for cats)

**Tip:** When searching on Apartments.com, Zillow, or Zumper, use the "Pet Friendly" filter to narrow results.

## 5. Parking Reality in SF

Parking in central SF is one of your biggest challenges:
- **In-building parking** at apartments typically costs **$300–$500/month** extra
- **Street parking** is extremely competitive, often requires residential permits, and may have time limits
- **Independent garages** near downtown cost $300–$600/month for monthly passes
- **Consider:** Do you truly need a car in SF? Public transit (Muni, BART) and rideshares may be more cost-effective for a 2–3 month stay. Selling or storing your car could save $400–$600/month.

## 8. Recommended Action Plan

1. **Start with Hawkins (77 Bruton St)** or **1288 Howard St** — these are the most budget-friendly central options. Contact them about cat policies, parking availability, and short-term lease options.

2. **Get 2 roommates** — this drops your rent to $1,315–$1,747 at most properties, leaving room in your $2,200 budget for utilities, parking, and pet costs.

3. **Prioritize all-inclusive listings** — platforms like Anyplace.com bundle utilities and WiFi into rent, eliminating surprise costs.

4. **Reconsider the car** — parking alone could eat $300–$500/month. SF has excellent public transit. If you can manage without the car, you'll save significantly.

5. **Neighborhood pick:** If you want nightlife and meeting people → **Marina or SoMa**. If you want the safest, quietest, most affordable option → **Inner Sunset**. If you want maximum downtown convenience → **SoMa or Civic Center**.
