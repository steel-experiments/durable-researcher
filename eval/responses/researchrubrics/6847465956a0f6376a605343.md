# 🎓 Student Group End-of-Year Banquet Plan — Berkeley, CA

---

## Executive Summary

This plan recommends **The Berkeley City Club** as the venue and **Humbowl** as the caterer for a 30-person banquet within a $2,500–$3,000 budget. The venue provides an elegant, historic atmosphere with event spaces suitable for presentations, and the caterer's inherently gluten-free, dairy-free, fully customizable rice bowl menu can safely accommodate all six dietary restrictions in the group.

---

## 🏛️ Venue: The Berkeley City Club

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| **Address** | 2315 Durant Ave, Berkeley, CA 94704 |
| **Type** | Historic landmark hotel with ballrooms and versatile event spaces |
| **Features** | Spacious ballrooms, stages, grand pianos; elegant Julia Morgan architecture |
| **Capacity** | Suitable for 30 guests (multiple room sizes available) |
| **Estimated Cost** | ~$500–$800 for an evening event (confirm directly) |

**Why this venue:** The Berkeley City Club is a stunning Julia Morgan-designed building just blocks from campus. It offers private event spaces with a banquet atmosphere appropriate for an end-of-year celebration. The venue hosts weddings, private dinners, and corporate events regularly.

> ⚠️ **Action Required:** Contact the Berkeley City Club directly to confirm (1) availability of a projector and screen for slides in the reserved room, and (2) exact room rental pricing. If a projector is not built-in, a portable projector can be rented for ~$50–$75 from local AV rental companies (e.g., Classic Party Rentals in Oakland).

**Backup Venue Option:** International House Berkeley (2299 Piedmont Ave) — near campus with large event spaces known to have built-in AV/projector equipment. Estimated half-day rental ~$1,000, which would reduce the food budget.

## 📋 Proposed Menu

### Format: Build-Your-Own Rice Bowl Buffet Catering

Each guest chooses a base, protein, and toppings. Individual bowls are assembled to order or pre-selected via group ordering to guarantee allergen safety.

#### Base Options (all inherently GF/DF)
- White jasmine rice
- Brown rice
- Mixed greens / salad base

#### Protein Options
| Protein | Egg-Free | Peanut-Free | Nut-Free | Dairy-Free | Shellfish-Free | Vegetarian | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hodo Foods organic tofu | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **Safe for ALL restrictions** |
| Slow-roasted legumes | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **Safe for ALL restrictions** |
| Chicken | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | Safe for all allergy profiles |
| Shrimp | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | Only for unrestricted guests |

#### Sauce Options (verify each with allergen matrix)
- Sesame-ginger dressing (egg-free, peanut-free, nut-free, dairy-free, vegan)
- Chili-lime vinaigrette (egg-free, peanut-free, nut-free, dairy-free, vegan)
- Teriyaki glaze (confirm nut/peanut-free with kitchen)
- **Avoid:** Any peanut-based satay sauce (peanut allergy trigger)

#### Toppings & Sides
- Fresh seasonal vegetables (roasted broccoli, pickled carrots, edamame, corn, cucumber)
- Avocado slices
- Sesame seeds (confirm no cross-contamination with nuts)
- Crispy shallots
- Pickled ginger
- **Avoid:** Crushed peanut topping, any egg-based dressings/mayo

#### Beverages
- Sparkling water and still water
- Iced tea (unsweetened and sweetened)
- Lemonade

#### Dessert
- Seasonal fruit platter (universally safe)

## 🛡️ Dietary Accommodation Matrix

| Guest Profile | # of Guests | Safe Proteins | Key Avoidances | Special Bowl Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **No restrictions** | 24 | Chicken, shrimp, tofu, or legumes | None | Any combination |
| **Egg allergy** | 2 | Tofu, legumes, or chicken | Eggs, egg-based sauces/mayo | No egg-containing sauces or toppings |
| **Peanut allergy** | 2 | Tofu, legumes, or chicken | Peanuts, peanut sauce, satay | No peanut toppings or peanut-based sauces |
| **Dairy + Nut + Shellfish allergy** | 1 | Tofu or legumes only | Dairy, all tree nuts, shellfish, peanut cross-contam | Dedicated vegan bowl — tofu or legume base, verify no nut cross-contamination |
| **Vegetarian** | 1 | Tofu or legumes | Meat, fish | Tofu or legume bowl with vegetables |

> **Note:** The guest with combined dairy + nut + shellfish allergy and the vegetarian guest should receive dedicated, separately prepared bowls to prevent any cross-contamination. Communicate this clearly to Humbowl's catering team using their allergen matrix.
