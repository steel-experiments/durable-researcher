# Clash Royale F2P Trophy Push Guide: 5200 → 6500 Trophies in 90 Days

*For King Level 40 free-to-play players.*

---

## The Math: What You're Up Against

You need to climb roughly **1,300 net trophies in ~90 days**, which breaks down to about **14–15 net trophies per day**. That's achievable for an F2P player, but only if you're disciplined about three things: **what deck you play, how you spend resources, and how you manage your sessions.**

The good news: the trophy system above 5000 features **permanent progression with arena floors** [1]. Once you cross into a new arena (e.g., hitting 5500), you can never drop below that threshold again. There are no aggressive monthly resets on Trophy Road—the old League system was moved to a separate Path of Legends ranked mode [1]. Every trophy you earn is yours to keep.

The challenge: matchmaking above 5000 no longer factors in King Level [1], meaning you'll face opponents with potentially higher-level cards. You need to outplay them through fundamentals, not brute force.

## Part 1: Choose ONE Deck and Commit

This is the single most important decision you'll make. As an F2P player, you cannot afford to split upgrade resources across multiple archetypes. A Level 13→14 upgrade costs **100,000 Gold per card** [5]. Spread that across even two decks and you'll be underleveled everywhere.

### Recommended Primary Deck: Hog Earthquake Cycle (3.3 avg elixir)

| Card | Role |
|---|---|
| Hog Rider | Win condition (tower damage) |
| Earthquake | Building clear + chip damage |
| Firecracker | Lane defense + counter-push |
| Valkyrie | Ground swarm killer + tank |
| Tesla | Versatile air+ground defense |
| Skeletons | Cheap cycle + distraction |
| Ice Spirit | Cycle + freeze value |
| The Log | Spell utility + swarm clear |

**Why this deck for F2P:**
- **Common/Rare heavy.** No Champions, no Evolutions required. Nearly every card is easy to request from clanmates and cheap to upgrade.
- **Cycle mechanic overcomes level gaps.** Because you're cycling cheap cards, you always have an answer. A Level 11 Hog still does meaningful tower damage across multiple hits.
- **Favorable meta matchups.** It hard-counters X-Bow cycle (roughly 75% win rate) and holds even against Royal Giant bait and Log Bait (~50-55% WR) [2].
- **Substitution flexibility.** If card levels are an issue: Musketeer can replace Firecracker; Knight can replace Valkyrie [2].

### Backup Option: Hog 2.6 Cycle (2.75 avg elixir)

Hog Rider, Ice Golem, Musketeer, Cannon, Ice Spirit, Skeletons, The Log, Fireball. An even cheaper cycle that's been a competitive staple for years. It relies purely on out-cycling opponents through fundamental mechanics rather than card stats [3].

### What to Avoid

Steer clear of expensive archetypes—PEKKA Bridge Spam (4.0 avg), Lava Hound (3.9), Golem beatdown. These require multiple high-level Epics, Legendaries, Champions, and Evolutions to function. At your card levels, they'll underperform [2][3].

**Smart Gem use:** Save Gems for **Classic/Grand Challenges**. Cards are normalized to Level 11 in challenges, eliminating your card-level disadvantage entirely [6]:
- Mega Draft Challenge (50 Gems): 12 wins = 11,000 Gold + 550 Cards

### Upgrade Order Within Your Deck

1. **Win condition** (Hog Rider) — max this first
2. **Defensive core** (Tesla, Valkyrie) — keeps you alive
3. **Spells** (Earthquake, Log) — damage scales with level
4. **Cycle cards** (Skeletons, Ice Spirit) — least level-dependent

## Part 4: Gameplay Strategy for Mid-Ladder Climbing

### Session Management

- **Stop after 3 consecutive losses.** The arena floor system protects you, but tilt losses are real. Take a 30-minute break and come back fresh [1].
- **Play in focused 30–45 minute sessions** rather than marathons. Mental fatigue leads to elixir mismanagement.
- **Target 20–30 minutes/day minimum** for your daily wins and donations, then play more when you're feeling sharp.

### Core Principles at 5200+ Trophies

**1. Elixir advantage is everything.** Never make the first move at the start of a game unless you have a clear read. Wait for your opponent to commit, defend efficiently, then counter-push with Hog.

**2. Track their spells.** In Hog Earthquake, your opponent's Log/Fireball/Arrows are your biggest enemy. If they just used their splash spell on Firecracker, immediately punish with a Hog on the opposite lane.

**3. Opposite-lane pressure.** If they build a big push on one side, drop your Hog on the other. They'll have to either split elixir (weakening their push) or take free tower damage.

**4. Earthquake value.** Don't waste Earthquake on just one building. Save it for when they've committed a Tesla, Cannon, or Bomb Tower, ideally when troops are clustered nearby for the splash damage.

**5. Firecracker lane control.** Place Firecracker behind your tower on the side you want to protect. Her ricochet clears swarms and chips the opposing tower simultaneously.

### Handling Level Disadvantages

You will face higher-level cards. Cycle decks mitigate this because:
- Your Hog only needs to connect 4–5 times to take a tower, regardless of level
- Cheap defense (Skeletons + Ice Spirit) handles most threats at any level
- You cycle back to your answers faster than they can cycle to their threats
