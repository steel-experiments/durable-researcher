# 🇹🇭 9-Day Thailand Itinerary for 7 Travelers from Hyderabad
**Budget: ₹70,000/person (₹18,000 flights + ₹52,000 on-ground)**

---

## Executive Summary

A logistically tight route through **Bangkok → Ayutthaya → Chiang Mai → Pai** delivers cultural immersion, adventure, nightlife, and 2+ days of rural countryside — all within ₹52,000 per person on the ground. By leveraging overnight train travel (saving one hotel night), budget dorms and group Airbnbs, and Thailand's legendary street food, the estimated on-ground spend comes to **~₹40,250/person**, leaving a healthy **₹11,750 buffer** for nightlife, shopping, or spontaneous activities.

## ⚠️ Practical Tips for the Group

1. **Visa:** Indian citizens need a Thai visa. Apply for Visa on Arrival (฿2,000 / ~₹5,000 — budget from your buffer) or a Tourist Visa from the Thai Embassy in Hyderabad (~₹2,500).

2. **Currency Exchange:** Carry ~฿5,000 in cash; use ATMs in Bangkok/Chiang Mai (฿220 withdrawal fee). Avoid airport exchange counters — rates are poor.

3. **SIM Card:** Buy an AIS or DTAC tourist SIM at the airport (฿299-599 for 7-10 days unlimited data). Essential for Grab rides and Google Maps.

4. **Grab App:** Download Grab (Southeast Asia's Uber) for reliable, metered transport in Bangkok and Chiang Mai. Cheaper than flagging taxis.

5. **Group of 7 Logistics:** Always split into one tuk-tuk (max 3-4) + one songthaew (max 6-8) for local transport. For intercity, book train tickets 3-5 days in advance — sleeper berths sell out.

6. **Street Food Safety:** Eat at busy stalls (high turnover = fresh food). Avoid ice at small stalls unless it's tubular (factory-made). Always drink bottled water.

7. **Temple Dress Code:** Carry a lightweight shawl/scarf. Shoulders and knees must be covered at all temples — no exceptions.

8. **Book Elephant Nature Park Early:** It's the most popular ethical sanctuary and books out 2-3 weeks ahead. Reserve online at elephantnaturepark.org.
