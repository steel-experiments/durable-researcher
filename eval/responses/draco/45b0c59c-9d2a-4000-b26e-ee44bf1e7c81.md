# Relocation Comparison: Austin vs. Raleigh vs. Nashville
### 10-Year Financial & Quality-of-Life Analysis for a Senior Mechanical Engineer

---

## Executive Summary

**Raleigh emerges as the strongest overall choice**, offering the best balance of affordable housing near quality schools, a moderate total tax burden, and deep engineering job market access. While Austin provides the highest-rated individual school districts and Nashville offers no income tax, Raleigh's combination of sub-$435K metro-wide median home prices, the lowest property tax burden (~0.64% of home value), and proximity to the Research Triangle's aerospace/manufacturing corridor makes it the most financially advantageous destination over a 10-year horizon for your family profile.

## 1. Salary & Job Market Depth

### BLS Regional Wage Data (Mechanical Engineers, May 2024)

The **national median annual wage** for mechanical engineers was **$102,320** as of May 2024, with employment projected to grow **9% from 2024–2034** — much faster than the national average for all occupations. Approximately **18,100 openings** are projected annually nationwide [1].

| Factor | Austin, TX | Raleigh, NC | Nashville, TN |
|---|---|---|---|
| National median (baseline) | $102,320 | $102,320 | $102,320 |
| Expected senior-level range | $95K–$120K | $90K–$115K | $88K–$110K |
| Key aerospace/manufacturing employers | Tesla, Samsung, Lockheed Martin, Applied Materials | IBM, Red Hat (engineering divisions), Siemens, nearby aerospace/defense in RTP | Nissan, Bridgestone, General Motors |
| Metro household income | $98,508 [4] | $96,096 [4] | Est. ~$85K–$95K |

**Analysis:** At your current $95K salary, you are slightly below the national median for the occupation. All three metros support comparable or slightly higher senior mechanical engineering salaries. Austin's tech-heavy economy likely commands the highest ceiling due to Tesla's Gigafactory and semiconductor expansion (Samsung's $17B fab in Taylor, TX). Raleigh benefits from the Research Triangle Park ecosystem with defense/aerospace contractors. Nashville's manufacturing base (automotive) is solid but thinner for aerospace specifically.

**Job Market Depth Verdict:** Austin > Raleigh > Nashville for aerospace/manufacturing postings, though Raleigh's RTP corridor is competitive and more diversified across engineering disciplines.

**Total Tax Verdict:** **Raleigh** < Nashville < Austin for your income bracket and home price range.

## 5. K–5 Public School Performance

### Austin Metro [3]
- **Eanes ISD**: 6 of the top 8 elementary schools in the metro. Student-teacher ratios of 10:1–12:1. Schools include Eanes Elementary, Cedar Creek, Barton Creek, Bridge Point, Forest Trail, Valley View — all rated A+ by Niche.
- **Round Rock ISD**: Cactus Ranch, Chandler Oaks, Sommer, Spicewood, Herrington, Laurel Mountain — strong A-rated options with 14:1–15:1 ratios.
- **Leander ISD**: River Ridge, Laura Welch Bush, Rutledge — A-rated, 12:1–16:1 ratios.
- **Dripping Springs ISD**: Sycamore Springs — solid A-rated option.

### Raleigh Metro
- **Wake County Public Schools** (includes Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Morrisville): One of the largest and highest-performing districts in the Southeast. Cary and Apex elementary schools consistently rate 8+/10 on GreatSchools. Notable schools include Salem Elementary, Weatherstone Elementary, Highcroft Drive Elementary.
- **Johnston County Schools**: Identified as offering strong academics at below-metro-median prices [2].
- **Chapel Hill-Carrboro City Schools**: Among the top districts in the state, though commuting to industrial zones may exceed 30 minutes.

### Nashville Metro
- **Williamson County Schools** (Brentwood, Franklin): The gold standard for Nashville-area K–5 education. Schools like Crockett Elementary, Kenrose Elementary, and Scales Elementary consistently achieve 9–10/10 ratings.
- **Davidson County/Metro Nashville**: More variable; top-performing schools concentrated in west Nashville (Julia Green, Percy Priest) but with higher variability across the district.

**School Quality Verdict:** Austin offers the deepest bench of top-rated elementary schools across multiple districts. **Raleigh** and **Nashville** (Williamson County) both offer excellent K–5 options, with Raleigh's Wake County system being more consistently strong across the entire county versus Nashville's concentration in Williamson County only.

Raleigh delivers the strongest 10-year financial outcome for your specific situation because:

1. **Lowest total tax burden** — The combination of moderate income tax (4.5%) and the nation's lowest effective property tax rate (0.64%) saves you an estimated **$38,000+ over 10 years** compared to Austin.

**Your next step**: Target home searches in **Cary (27511, 27513, 27519 zip codes)** or **Apex (27502, 27523)**, both offering 8+/10 elementary schools within 20 minutes of Research Triangle Park employers, at prices ranging from approximately $450K–$600K.
