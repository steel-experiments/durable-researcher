### Detailed Findings

**What is known about the park:**
- **Marriott's Great America** in Santa Clara, California opened on March 20, 1976, as one of two identical Marriott theme parks (the other in Gurnee, Illinois). The park later became Paramount's Great America, then California's Great America under Cedar Fair, and was acquired by Six Flags in 2024. It is expected to close after the 2027 season after the land was sold to Prologis for $310 million in 2022. [1]
