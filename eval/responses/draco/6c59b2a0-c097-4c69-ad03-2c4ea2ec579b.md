### The Answer

The chain is **Red Rooster**, and the long-running lunchtime promotion is called the **"Quarter Chicken & Chips"** lunch deal.

---

### How It All Matches Up

**The chain:** Red Rooster was founded in **1972** by Greek-Australian brothers **Peter and Theo Kailis** in **Kelmscott**, a suburb of Perth, Western Australia. The Kailis family had made their name in fishing and pearling but pivoted to roast chicken to compete with the American chains (KFC had arrived in Australia in 1968). Their distinctive **red signage** became one of the most recognised brand marks in Western Australian fast food. By 1981 the chain had grown large enough that Myer Emporium bought it for $8.97 million — it was already Australia's fourth-largest fast food group.

**The promotion:** The **Quarter Chicken & Chips** deal has been running since at least the 1990s. It offers exactly what it says — a quarter roast chicken and a serve of their signature seasoned chips — for just a few dollars. Around **2015** the price sat in the **$4.95–$5.95** range. The deal is strictly a daytime offer, available from opening (around 9 AM) and **cut off mid-afternoon** (historically 3:00–3:30 PM; more recently 4:00 PM). It's in-store and Click & Collect only — never available for delivery.

The price has crept up with inflation over the decades ($5.95 in 2019, $6.95 in 2021, $7.95 in 2022–23, $8.95 by 2024), but the essential formula hasn't changed: cheap chicken and chips, lunchtime only.
