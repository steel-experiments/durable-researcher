## Final Report: Université Paris-Saclay Master's in Economics & France Excellence Charpak Master's Scholarship (as of November 2025)

---

### Executive Summary

The Master's in Economics at Université Paris-Saclay is a selective, two-year research-oriented program taught entirely in English, requiring a relevant bachelor's degree and strong English proficiency. The France Excellence Charpak Master's Scholarship, administered by Campus France India on behalf of the French Embassy, offers Indian citizens up to €860/month and extensive benefits, with the 2026 campaign deadline set for **30 March 2026**.

---

### Part 1: Master's in Economics — Université Paris-Saclay

#### Program Overview
The Master's in Economics is a research-level program designed to prepare students for PhD studies. It is taught **entirely in English** and delivered on the **Saclay Moulon campus** (Saclay / Gif-sur-Yvette / Orsay). The contact person is Natacha Raffin (natacha.raffin@ens-paris-saclay.fr). [1][3]

#### Curriculum Structure
- **M1 (Year 1):** 8 compulsory core courses (microeconomics, macroeconomics, applied econometrics), 2 optional discovery courses, and 2 research projects.
- **M2 (Year 2):** Entirely optional modules under faculty supervision, allowing specialization in one of three tracks:
  1. Trade, Geography and Development Economics
  2. Public and Behavioral Economics
  3. Advances and Challenges in Macroeconomics
- **Capstone:** A Research Master thesis demonstrating original, independent research. [1][3][5]

#### Admission Requirements & Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Prior education** | A Bachelor's degree (3 or 4 years) or equivalent of **180 ECTS credits** in Economics, Mathematics, or a closely related quantitative field. [4][5] |
| **Language proficiency** | High level of English required (typically **B2 or C1** on the CEFR scale; TOEFL/IELTS scores accepted). French proficiency is not required but considered a plus. [4][5][6] |
| **Selectivity** | Admission is **selective** and places are limited. [2] |

#### Application Process & Required Documents

- **Platform:** International candidates apply through the **Études en France (EEF)** platform or the university's **INCEPTION** portal. [2][4]
- **Deadlines:** Vary by program. Typically, international admissions open in **late autumn** and close between **February and April** for a September intake. Exact dates are published on individual course pages. [2][4]
- **Typical required documents** (based on university-wide standards and program context):
  - Official academic transcripts and Bachelor's degree certificate (or proof of ongoing studies)
  - Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  - Statement of Purpose / Cover letter
  - Letters of recommendation (typically two)
  - Proof of English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS)
  - Copy of passport [4][6]

- **Post-admission steps:** Admission confirmation on the platform, CVEC (Student and Campus Life Contribution) payment, administrative registration (document verification), and academic registration (course selection). [2]

### Part 2: France Excellence Charpak Master's Scholarship

This scholarship is funded by the French Embassy in India and administered by **Campus France India**. It supports Indian students pursuing a full-time Master's degree in France, including at Université Paris-Saclay. [7][8]

#### Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Nationality** | Must be an **Indian citizen** or hold an **OCI card**. [7] |
| **Age** | **30 years old or younger** at the time of application. [7] |
| **Education** | Currently enrolled in or a graduate of a higher education institution. **Preference is given to degrees from Indian institutions** over foreign ones. [7] |
| **Program type** | Must enroll in a **full-time Master's** (1 or 2 years) in France. **Not** valid for PhD, semester exchanges, internships, or standalone research projects. [7] |
| **Location** | The funded course must take place in France. [7] |
| **Prior French study** | Students already in M1 in France **may apply for M2** funding. [7] |
| **Previous recipients** | Past Charpak Master scholarship recipients are **ineligible**. [7] |
| **Language** | French language skills are an **asset but not mandatory**. [7] |
| **Admission status** | Applicants **must have already applied** for admission to a French higher education institution before submitting the scholarship application. [7] |

#### Scholarship Benefits

Successful awardees receive **"Boursier du Gouvernement Français"** status, which includes:

- **Monthly living allowance:** €860
- **Exemption** from visa fees and Campus France procedure fees
- **Student social security** and additional health insurance (*mutuelle*)
- **Accommodation assistance:** priority access to CROUS student residences
- **Subsidized meals:** university restaurant meals at **€1 each**
- **Exemption from differentiated enrollment fees** (subject to the institution's policies)
- **Note:** The scholarship does **not** cover travel expenses to or within France. [7]

#### Required Documents

All documents must be uploaded in **PDF or JPEG** format to the online scholarship portal:

1. Formal passport-size photograph
2. Copy of passport's first page (or proof of passport application)
3. Curriculum Vitae (maximum **2 pages**)
4. Admission/acceptance letter from the French institution (or email correspondence proving the application process is underway)
5. Mark sheets and degree certificates for: Higher Secondary School (XII), Bachelor's, and Master's (if applicable)
6. French language certificate (e.g., DELF/DALF), if applicable
7. Employment/internship record, if applicable
8. **Recommendation letter(s)** from current Indian university/employer or last attended institution — must be typed, signed, on headed paper with a stamp, and include referee contact details [7]

#### Application Deadlines (2026 Campaign)

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| **Application deadline** | **30 March 2026 (23:59 IST)** |
| Interview period (Round 2) | 13 April – 30 April 2026 |
| Results publication | End of May 2026 |

#### Selection Process

- **Round 1:** Complete applications are evaluated by a selection committee on the basis of **academic excellence, consistency, quality of the Statement of Purpose (SOP), and overall profile**. There is **no minimum CGPA** required. A **plagiarism and AI-content check** is conducted on the SOP; significant use of AI tools results in **disqualification**. [7]
- **Round 2:** Shortlisted candidates are invited to an **online interview** (scheduled between 13–30 April 2026). Interview dates **cannot be rescheduled**. [7]
- The scholarship is **renewable once** for the second year (M2), subject to a request and satisfactory academic results during M1. [8]

### Uncertainty & Gaps

- **Specific M1 Economics admission criteria:** The Université Paris-Saclay website pages browsed did not display a fully detailed list of program-specific prerequisites (e.g., minimum GPA, exact TOEFL/IELTS scores, specific coursework requirements). The university directs applicants to the "Je candidate" portal and individual course pages for precise requirements. The bachelor's degree/180 ECTS requirement and B2/C1 English level are inferred from the broader university framework and program context rather than from an explicit, itemized list on the Economics page itself.
- **Application platform:** There is a slight ambiguity between the INCEPTION platform, Études en France (EEF), and the "Je candidate" portal. It appears that EEF is the primary channel for Indian students, while INCEPTION may serve other international applicants. Applicants should verify the correct platform based on their nationality.
- **2025 campaign deadlines for Charpak:** The detailed deadlines above (30 March 2026) are from the **2026 campaign**. The 2025 campaign deadlines (for the 2025–2026 academic year) would have already passed by November 2025. Historical patterns suggest deadlines fall in late March to early April annually.
