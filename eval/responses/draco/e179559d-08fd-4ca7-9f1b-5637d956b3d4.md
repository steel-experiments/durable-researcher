## 1. Fund Overview

### Edelweiss Greater China Equity Offshore Fund
- **Category:** Greater China Equity (Thematic/International)
- **Structure:** Open-ended Fund of Fund (Feeder) — invests in an overseas fund focused on Greater China equities (China A-shares, Hong Kong-listed, Taiwan-listed companies)
- **Underlying Fund:** JPMorgan Funds – JF Greater China Equity Fund (historically)
- **Launch Date:** ~2018
- **Benchmark:** MSCI Golden Dragon Index / MSCI China Index variants

### DSP Global Innovation Overseas Equity Omni FoF
- **Category:** Global/International Fund of Fund
- **Structure:** Open-ended FoF that invests in the DSP Global Innovation Fund (an overseas equity fund), which in turn invests in a portfolio of global innovation-themed equities
- **Underlying Theme:** Innovation and disruption across technology, healthcare, fintech, and other secular growth sectors globally
- **Launch Date:** ~2021
- **Benchmark:** MSCI ACWI (All Country World Index) or similar global benchmark

## 4. Expense Ratios

| Metric | Edelweiss Greater China | DSP Global Innovation Omni FoF |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct Plan TER** | ~1.00–1.25% | ~0.75–1.00% (FoF level) + underlying fund expenses |
| **Regular Plan TER** | ~1.75–2.25% | ~1.50–2.00% (FoF level) + underlying fund expenses |
| **Effective Total Cost** | Single layer (feeder into one fund) | Double layer (FoF + underlying global fund) |

**Important Nuance:** The DSP FoF has a compounded expense ratio because it charges its own TER *on top of* the underlying fund's expenses. The Edelweiss feeder structure also has layered costs but typically with only one underlying fund. The **effective total expense ratio of the DSP FoF is likely higher** (estimated 1.5–2.0% all-in) compared to the Edelweiss fund (~1.0–1.5% all-in). This is a meaningful drag on long-term compounding.

## 6. Suitability Analysis by Investment Horizon

### For a 2-Year Investment Horizon → **DSP Global Innovation Overseas Equity Omni FoF**

**Rationale:**
- **Lower near-term risk:** Global diversification across 15+ countries and multiple sectors reduces the probability of catastrophic loss over a short horizon.
- **Favorable macro backdrop:** AI/innovation themes remain in a secular uptrend with strong earnings momentum from US mega-cap tech. This tailwind is likely to persist over a 2-year window.
- **China uncertainty:** The Edelweiss Greater China fund is subject to extreme policy risk (Beijing's regulatory whim, US-China tensions, property sector unresolved issues). Over just 2 years, a single negative geopolitical or policy event could cause severe drawdowns.
- **Drawback:** Higher effective expense ratio is a modest drag over 2 years but is outweighed by the risk-mitigation benefit of diversification.

### For a 5-Year Investment Horizon → **Edelweiss Greater China Equity Offshore Fund** (with conviction) OR **DSP Global Innovation FoF** (for most investors)

**This depends on investor risk appetite:**

**Case for Edelweiss Greater China (contrarian/high-risk):**
- Chinese equities are trading at historically depressed valuations (MSCI China P/E of ~8–10x vs. MSCI US at ~20–22x). Over a 5-year horizon, mean reversion could generate outsized returns if China's structural issues are resolved.
- The 2024 stimulus was a first step; structural reforms over 2025–2029 could unlock significant value.
- Historical evidence shows Chinese equities can deliver 50–100% rallies in short windows after prolonged slumps.
- **However:** 5 years may still not be enough if Japan-style stagnation sets in. This is a high-conviction, high-risk bet.

**Case for DSP Global Innovation FoF (prudent/balanced):**
- Proven secular themes (AI, biotech, clean energy, digital transformation) are multi-decade trends that compound well over 5+ years.
- Geographic diversification protects against single-country black swan events.
- The compounding effect of global innovation leaders has historically been powerful over 5-year windows.
- The higher expense ratio is more easily absorbed over a longer horizon by superior risk-adjusted returns.

**Recommendation:**
- **Conservative/moderate investor with 5-year horizon:** DSP Global Innovation FoF — better risk-adjusted profile, diversified, rides proven secular themes.
- **Aggressive/contrarian investor with 5-year horizon:** Edelweiss Greater China — asymmetric upside if Chinese equities mean-revert, but must accept potential for further downside.
