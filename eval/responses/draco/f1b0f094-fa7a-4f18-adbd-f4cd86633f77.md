# Relocation Comparison: Toowoomba vs Bundaberg vs Cairns for Semi-Retirement Transition

## Executive Summary

**Toowoomba emerges as the optimal choice**, offering the largest cost-of-living differential versus Melbourne (housing 42-59% cheaper, utilities 60% cheaper), established cardiac care infrastructure within the city, and a regional economy large enough to support part-time accounting work. Cairns is a strong runner-up with better salary parity and excellent cardiac services but higher property prices. Bundaberg offers the cheapest rent but has the weakest healthcare depth and smallest employment market.

## 3. Cardiac Care Access (Atrial Fibrillation Management)

This is a critical factor given managed atrial fibrillation requiring ongoing specialist access within 90 minutes.

### Toowoomba
- **Toowoomba Hospital** (public, Queensland Health): Major regional referral hospital serving the Darling Downs region. Has a cardiology department with specialist physicians. St Andrew's Toowoomba Private Hospital also offers emergency cardiac services and cardiac procedures.
- **Proximity:** Both facilities are within the Toowoomba city area — essentially 0–30 minutes from anywhere in the city.
- **Brisbane access:** 90 minutes by road to Brisbane's major cardiac centres (Prince Charles Hospital, RBWH, Greenslopes Private) for complex interventions — **exactly at the 90-minute threshold**.
- **Assessment: STRONG.** Local cardiac services plus Brisbane backup.

### Cairns
- **Cairns Hospital Cardiology Clinic** (public): Full cardiology outpatient clinic at 165 Esplanade, Cairns North. Operates Mon–Fri with fee-based services. Provides cardiology investigations and management.
- **Cairns Private Hospital:** Offers cardiac services.
- **Cairns Cardiology** (private practice): Established since 1992, providing both invasive and non-invasive cardiovascular services. Multiple cardiologists on staff. Serves patients from Far North Queensland, PNG, and Torres Strait Islands — indicating significant depth of service. Has satellite clinic in Innisfail.
- **Proximity:** All within Cairns city — 0–30 minutes access.
- **Townsville backup:** ~4.5 hours by road; generally accessible by air/Royal Flying Doctor for emergencies.
- **Assessment: STRONG.** Comprehensive local services with multiple cardiologists and both public and private options. However, backup specialist centre (Townsville) is distant.

### Bundaberg
- **Bundaberg Cardiology** at Friendly Society Private Hospital: Comprehensive cardiac service including diagnostic, therapeutic, and surgical procedures. Has a dedicated Cardiac Cath Lab, Coronary Care Unit, and Cardiac Step Down Unit. Staffed by at least 4 cardiologists (Dr Andre Conradie, Dr Hermann Wittmer, Dr David Grout, Dr Ahmad Zakariyya). Partnership with GenesisCare.
- **Bundaberg Hospital** (public, Queensland Health): Also provides cardiology services.
- **Proximity:** Within Bundaberg city — 0–20 minutes.
- **Assessment: GOOD but with caveats.** The Friendlies provides excellent cardiac infrastructure for a regional city. However, with 4 cardiologists total, depth is more limited than Cairns or the Toowoomba-Brisbane corridor. For routine AF management (consultations, echocardiograms, Holter monitoring, medication review), this is more than adequate. For complex interventions (catheter ablation), a trip to Brisbane (~4 hours) or Sunshine Coast (~2 hours) may be needed.

### Cardiac Care Ranking
1. **Toowoomba** — Good local services + 90-min access to Brisbane's world-class cardiac centres
2. **Cairns** — Comprehensive standalone services but geographically isolated from tertiary backup
3. **Bundaberg** — Good local private facility but smaller specialist pool; nearest major centre 2+ hours away

## 6. Recommendation: Toowoomba

**Toowoomba is the optimal choice** for your specific circumstances. Here's why:

1. **Maximum cost savings** — At ~$23,000–$25,000/year cheaper than Melbourne, Toowoomba delivers the largest financial runway for your 8-year pre-retirement window. Over 8 years, this could mean ~$185,000–$200,000 in savings.

2. **Cardiac safety net** — Your managed atrial fibrillation is well-served by local cardiology services in Toowoomba, with the critical advantage of Brisbane's world-class cardiac hospitals (Prince Charles, RBWH) reachable within 90 minutes. This is the only city of the three offering both local care AND rapid access to a major tertiary cardiac centre.

3. **Employment sustainability** — With ~170,000 population, a university, a major hospital, RAAF base, and thriving agribusiness sector, Toowoomba's economy is large enough to support part-time corporate accounting roles at $35+/hour. The city's growing status as a commuter satellite of Brisbane also opens hybrid work possibilities.

4. **Property within budget** — $550,000 buys a quality 3-bedroom home in established suburbs like Glenvale, Harristown, or Wilsonton. The market has plateaued after 2020–2022 growth, offering stable entry pricing without bubble risk.

5. **Lifestyle transition** — Toowoomba's temperate climate (elevation ~700m) is more moderate than tropical Cairns or humid Bundaberg, which may suit someone accustomed to Melbourne. The city has a strong café culture, gardens, and cultural amenities that ease the transition from a capital city.

**Secondary recommendation:** If tropical lifestyle is a personal priority and you can accept tighter property budget and geographic isolation from tertiary cardiac backup, **Cairns** is a viable alternative with surprisingly strong salary levels and comprehensive local cardiac services.

**Caution on Bundaberg:** While cheapest on paper, Bundaberg's small employment market, limited specialist depth, and surprisingly high grocery costs make it the riskiest choice for someone balancing healthcare needs with income sustainability during semi-retirement.
