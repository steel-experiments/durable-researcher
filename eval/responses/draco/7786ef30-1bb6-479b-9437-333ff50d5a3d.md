# 3-Month Instagram Lead-Generation Roadmap
### For a Digital Marketing Service Provider Targeting Indian SMBs & Emerging Brands
**Monthly Budget: ₹40,000 | Duration: 3 Months (₹120,000 Total)**

---

## (a) Measurable Goals

### Monthly Targets (Steady-State by Month 2–3)

| Metric | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw Leads Generated | 30–40 | 50–60 | 60–75 |
| Qualified Leads (after filtering) | 15–20 | 25–35 | 30–40 |
| Target Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) | ₹600–₹800 | ₹450–₹600 | ₹400–₹500 |
| Discovery Calls Booked | 5–8 | 10–14 | 12–16 |
| Follower Growth | +200–400 | +400–600 | +500–800 |

**Rationale:** Instagram CPM in India for B2B audiences typically ranges ₹150–₹200. With a ₹30,000/month ad spend and a CPL of ₹400–₹600 for digital marketing services targeting SMBs, 50–75 raw leads per month is achievable. Factoring in a 50–60% qualification rate, this yields 25–40 qualified leads. Month 1 carries a learning premium (higher CPL) as audiences and creatives are tested. By Month 3, retargeting pools are larger and winning creatives are identified, driving CPL down.

## (b) 4 Content Pillars, Weekly Posting Cadence & 6 Reusable Templates

### Content Pillars

| # | Pillar | Purpose | % of Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Educational / Actionable Value** | Marketing tips, platform algorithm updates, "how-to" guides for SMB owners | 30% |
| 2 | **Case Studies / Social Proof** | Client ROI metrics, before-and-after results, testimonial highlights | 25% |
| 3 | **Relatable SMB Pain Points** | Agitating common marketing struggles ("Why your local ads aren't converting"), myth-busting | 25% |
| 4 | **Behind-the-Scenes / Soft Pitch** | Agency culture, day-in-the-life, service breakdowns, free audit CTAs | 20% |

### Weekly Posting Cadence

| Day | Content Type | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Reel (30–60 sec) | Educational Tip or Myth vs. Fact |
| **Wednesday** | Reel (30–60 sec) | Case Study or Pain Point Agitation |
| **Thursday** | Carousel (5–7 slides) | Educational Checklist or Service Breakdown |
| **Friday** | Reel (15–30 sec) | Behind-the-scenes / Trending audio / Relatable |
| **Daily** | 3–4 Stories | Polls, Q&A, reposts, behind-the-scenes, CTA slides |

**Total: 4 feed posts/week + 3–4 Stories/day = ~28–30 touchpoints/month**

### 6 Reusable Post Templates

1. **Hook → Problem → Solution → CTA Reel**
   - *Format:* 30-sec talking-head or text-on-screen with trending audio. Opens with a provocative hook ("Stop burning money on Meta ads"), states the problem, delivers a quick fix, ends with "DM me 'AUDIT' for a free review."
   - *Reuse:* Swap the hook/topic each week; structure stays identical.

2. **Case Study Carousel (Challenge → Strategy → Result)**
   - *Format:* 5-slide swipe-through. Slide 1: Bold client result headline ("How we 3x'd this café's weekend footfall"). Slides 2–3: Challenge & strategy. Slide 4: Metrics with a before/after visual. Slide 5: CTA.
   - *Reuse:* Replace data, niche, and screenshots each time.

3. **Myth vs. Fact Reel**
   - *Format:* Fast-cut 15–20 sec Reel with trending audio. Text overlay: "Myth: You need ₹1 lakh/month to run ads" → "Fact: We've scaled brands at ₹15K/month." CTA: "Save this for your next meeting."
   - *Reuse:* New myth each week from a running list of 20+ common misconceptions.

4. **POV / Relatable SMB Moment Reel**
   - *Format:* 10–15 sec comedic or empathetic clip. "POV: You just spent ₹20K on boosting posts and got 3 likes." No hard sell—pure engagement and shareability.
   - *Reuse:* Swap the scenario; keep the relatable tone.

5. **Client Testimonial Highlight**
   - *Format:* Static post or short Reel. Client quote as text overlay on branded background, or a 20-sec video clip of the client speaking. Add their logo and a one-line result metric.
   - *Reuse:* New client, same branded template.

6. **"5 Things to Check Before…" Checklist Carousel**
   - *Format:* 6-slide carousel. Slide 1: Title hook ("5 things to check before hiring a marketing agency"). Slides 2–6: One tip each with an icon. Final slide: CTA to DM for a free consultation.
   - *Reuse:* Change the topic (before running ads / before redesigning your website / before launching on Instagram, etc.).

## (d) Lead-Capture & Nurturing Workflow

### Lead-Capture Flow Diagram

```
Instagram Content (Reel/Carousel/Story)
        │
        ├── Organic Path: Viewer comments keyword ("AUDIT", "SCALE", "GROWTH")
        │       │
        │       ▼
        │   ManyChat auto-replies in DM, delivers lead magnet (PDF guide, 
        │   free audit link), collects email + phone number
        │       │
        │       ▼
        │   Lead data pushed to Zoho CRM via native integration or Zapier
        │
        └── Paid Path: User clicks Lead Gen Ad
                │
                ▼
            Meta Instant Form (pre-filled with name, email, phone)
                │
                ▼
            Lead data auto-synced to Zoho CRM via Meta–Zoho webhook
                │
                ▼
            Same nurturing sequence triggered (see below)
```

### DM Automation Setup (ManyChat)

- **Trigger 1 – Comment Keyword:** When a user comments "AUDIT" on any post, ManyChat sends: *"Hey [Name]! 👋 Thanks for your interest. Drop your email and I'll send you a free 5-point ad-account audit checklist + a link to book a free 15-min strategy call."*
- **Trigger 2 – Story Mention/DM:** When someone DMs the account or mentions it in a Story, auto-reply with a quick FAQ + link to Calendly.
- **Trigger 3 – New Follower:** Send a welcome message introducing the agency and offering a free resource.

*ManyChat Pro at $15/month (~₹1,250) is essential for unlimited keywords, tags, custom fields, and CRM integrations. The Free plan limits you to 3 keywords and 1,000 contacts, which is insufficient for lead gen at scale.*

### CRM Integration (Zoho CRM — Free Edition)

Zoho CRM's free tier supports up to 3 users and includes lead management, deals pipeline, workflow automation, reports, and a mobile app — all at ₹0/month. This is ideal for a solo creator or small agency.

**Setup Steps:**
1. Create a Zoho CRM account → set up a "Leads" module with fields: Name, Phone, Email, Source (Organic/Paid), Service Interest, Lead Score
2. Connect ManyChat → Zoho via native integration or Zapier (Zapier Free plan supports 100 tasks/month)
3. Connect Meta Lead Ads → Zoho via Meta webhook or Zapier
4. Create lead assignment rule: All new leads → "New" stage in pipeline

### 5-Day Follow-Up Sequence

| Timing | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 0 (Immediate)** | WhatsApp + Email | Automated welcome message: *"Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! Here's your free [resource]. I'd love to chat about your marketing — book a free 15-min call here: [Calendly link]"* |
| **Day 1** | WhatsApp | Personalized text: *"Hey [Name], did you get a chance to look at the guide? Happy to walk you through it on a quick call."* |
| **Day 2** | Phone Call | Manual follow-up call from the creator. Objective: Qualify the lead, understand their budget and pain points, pitch the discovery call. |
| **Day 4** | Email/DM | Value-add message: Share a relevant case study (*"Here's how we helped a similar [industry] business get [result]. Thought you'd find this useful."*) |
| **Day 7** | WhatsApp | Final touchpoint: *"Hey [Name], no pressure at all — just wanted to leave my calendar link open in case you'd like that free audit. [Link]. If the timing isn't right, totally understand!"* |

**Lead Scoring in Zoho:**
- Opened email (+1), Clicked Calendly link (+3), Booked a call (+5), Responded on WhatsApp (+2)
- Leads scoring 5+ → mark as "Hot"; score 2–4 → "Warm" (nurture with monthly content); score 0–1 → "Cold" (add to long-term email drip)

## (e) KPIs & Weekly Reporting Template

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Definition | Target (Month 3) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost Per Lead (CPL)** | Total ad spend ÷ total leads generated | ₹400–₹500 |
| **Lead Volume** | Total raw leads from organic + paid | 60–75/month |
| **Qualification Rate** | Qualified leads ÷ raw leads | 50–60% |
| **Lead-to-Call Rate** | Discovery calls booked ÷ qualified leads | 35–40% |
| **Call-to-Close Rate** | Clients signed ÷ discovery calls | 15–20% |
| **Engagement Rate** | (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) ÷ Reach | 3–5% |
| **Follower Growth Rate** | Net new followers ÷ total followers | 8–12%/month |
| **DM Reply Rate** | DMs received ÷ total post engagement | Track & improve |
| **Story Completion Rate** | Views on last Story slide ÷ first Story slide | >60% |
| **Cost Per Discovery Call** | Total spend ÷ calls booked | ₹1,000–₹1,500 |

## 3-Month Timeline at a Glance

### Month 1 — FOUNDATION & TESTING
- Set up ManyChat Pro, Zoho CRM, Canva Pro, and Meta Ads Manager
- Create and save all 6 reusable post templates
- Publish 16 feed posts + daily Stories
- Launch 3 ad campaigns (Lead Gen, Click-to-WA, Retargeting) with 4–6 audience tests
- Target: 30–40 raw leads, CPL ₹600–₹800
- Hire freelance video editor; onboard with template expectations

### Month 2 — OPTIMIZATION & ITERATION
- Kill underperforming ad sets; reallocate budget to top 2 audiences
- Introduce new creatives based on Month 1's top-performing organic content
- Build Lookalike Audience from Month 1 leads
- Refine ManyChat flows based on DM conversion data
- Target: 50–60 raw leads, CPL ₹450–₹600
- Start systematic lead follow-up via the 5-day sequence

### Month 3 — SCALING & SYSTEMIZATION
- Scale winning ad sets by 20–30% budget increase within the ₹30K cap
- Create retargeting pool from 2 months of engagers (larger audience = cheaper CPL)
- Introduce a lead magnet (e.g., "The SMB Meta Ads Playbook" PDF) to boost conversion rates
- Systemize reporting; identify seasonal content opportunities
- Target: 60–75 raw leads, CPL ₹400–₹500
- Evaluate if contractor hours need to increase (reallocate budget if needed)

### Key Assumptions & Risks

- **CPL of ₹400–600** is based on B2B service lead gen benchmarks for the Indian market. Actual CPL may vary by city tier, industry vertical, and creative quality. Month 1 will almost certainly be higher.
- **Zoho CRM Free** is sufficient for a solo operation but limited to 3 users and basic automation. Upgrade to Standard (₹800/user/month) if team grows.
- **ManyChat Free** is too restrictive (3 keywords, 1,000 contacts) for meaningful lead gen. The Pro plan at $15/month is non-negotiable.
- **Creative fatigue** is the biggest risk. With only 4 posts/week, each piece must perform. The 6 templates provide structure but require fresh angles/hooks weekly.
- **Freelancer dependency**: At ₹500–800/Reel, the quality of freelance editors varies. Test 2–3 editors in Month 1 before committing to one.
