### Executive Summary

Based on available Australian specifications, the **LG DVH9-09W** emerges as the strongest candidate for a Melbourne heritage terrace with limited ventilation, primarily due to its auto-cleaning condenser (critical for confined installations), lowest upfront price ($1,494 clearance vs. $2,399 RRP for the Bosch), unvented operation, and 9kg capacity suited to 6–8 weekly loads. The Bosch WTX88M20AU offers the highest energy star rating (10 stars) and established Australian parts support, but carries a significant price premium. The Samsung DV90T6240LH proved extremely difficult to locate in Australian retail channels, raising concerns about local availability and parts support.

### 2. Energy Consumption & Running Costs at Victorian Electricity Rates

**Victorian electricity rates** (2024–2025): Victorian default offer is approximately **28–32 cents/kWh** (flat rate) or **20–25 cents/kWh** (controlled load / off-peak). We'll use **30 cents/kWh** as a conservative flat-rate figure.

- Auto-cleaning condenser maintains efficiency over time, preventing the typical 10–20% degradation from lint buildup.
- **Annual energy cost ≈ $189–$231**
- **10-year energy cost ≈ $1,890–$2,310**

**Samsung DV90T6240LH:**
- Insufficient data to model precisely. A comparable 9 kg 9–10 star heat pump dryer would fall in a similar range to the LG.
- **Estimated 10-year energy cost ≈ $1,800–$2,300**

#### Key Insight on Condenser Maintenance & Efficiency

The LG's **auto-cleaning condenser** is a significant differentiator. In a heritage terrace with limited ventilation and high usage (6–8 loads/week), manually cleaned condensers (Bosch, Samsung) will accumulate lint faster. A dirty condenser can increase energy consumption by **15–25%** over time. If the Bosch condenser is cleaned only monthly (optimistic for a busy household), cumulative efficiency loss could add **$200–$400** over 10 years in excess energy costs.

### 6. Installation & Heritage Overlay Requirements

**Heritage overlay restrictions in Melbourne** (governed by individual council planning schemes under the Victoria Planning Provisions, typically Clause 43.01):

- **No external venting required:** This is the key advantage of ALL three models. Heat pump dryers are condenser-type — they don't require external wall vents. This avoids the need for any heritage permit for wall penetrations.
- **No structural modification:** All three plug into a standard 240V/10A power outlet. No plumbing is required for basic operation (though the LG's auto-cleaning condenser uses water from the cycle, and all models can optionally drain continuously via a hose).
- **Water drainage:** In a heritage terrace, you can either empty the water container manually or run the included drain hose to a nearby sink/laundry tub. No wall penetration needed.
- **Floor loading:** Heritage terrace floorboards may have limited load capacity. At ~50–55 kg, none of these units are excessively heavy, but a stable, level surface is essential.
- **Ventilation for ambient heat:** While heat pump dryers don't vent moist air, they do radiate some ambient heat. In a confined heritage terrace laundry space, ensure the room has a door gap or passive vent to prevent heat buildup. This does not require heritage approval.

**Note:** Always check with your local council's heritage advisor before any installation. Some councils may have specific requirements about laundry appliance placement visible from the street.

#### Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) Rebate

The Victorian Energy Upgrades program provides incentives for energy-efficient appliances. As of 2024–2025, heat pump dryers with high star ratings (9–10 stars) may qualify for VEEC (Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificates) incentives when purchased through participating retailers. The exact rebate amount varies but can be **$50–$200** for high-efficiency dryers. This should be confirmed with the retailer at time of purchase, as eligibility and amounts change periodically. All three models, being 9–10 star heat pump dryers, would likely qualify if the program covers clothes dryers in the current cycle.
