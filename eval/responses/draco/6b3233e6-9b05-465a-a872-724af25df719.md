# Farm Management & Traceability Platform Comparison for Smallholder Coffee Cooperatives

## Executive Summary

Based on research across the primary platforms identified—Olam's **AtSource**, FLOCERT's **FairTrace**, **SourceTrace**, and **Farmforce**—no single platform fully addresses all requirements of a 5,000-farmer cooperative spanning Colombia, Ethiopia, and Indonesia. AtSource is a buyer-facing proprietary tool tied to Olam's supply chain, not a licensable cooperative solution. FairTrace is a Fairtrade compliance transaction-verification system, not a field-level farm management tool. SourceTrace and Farmforce emerge as stronger candidates for field-level operations, though critical data gaps remain on pricing, SMS/basic-phone support, and GPS accuracy in mountainous terrain. A hybrid approach—combining a field-level platform with certification-specific tools—is likely the most viable path.

## Platform-by-Platform Analysis

### 1. AtSource (Olam / ofi)

**What it is:** AtSource is Olam's proprietary sustainable sourcing solution that provides traceability to product origin and transparency on supply chain sustainability challenges through data and insights. It includes a carbon and water footprint calculator (two tiers: "AtSourceV" using country-level data, and "AtSource+" using primary data). [[1]](https://www.atsource.io)

**Critical limitation for your cooperative:** AtSource is fundamentally a **buyer-facing** tool designed for Olam's corporate customers to trace products, monitor sustainability KPIs, and report to stakeholders. It is embedded within Olam's own supply chain operations—it is **not a standalone SaaS product** that an independent cooperative can license and deploy for its members.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Usability for basic phones | ❌ No evidence of SMS/USSD interface; appears to be a web-based dashboard for corporate buyers |
| GPS plot mapping | ❌ Not mentioned as a field-level mapping tool |
| Certification integration | ✅ Designed for sustainability reporting and due diligence; can support compliance frameworks |
| Multi-language | ⚠️ Not documented publicly |
| Offline functionality | ❌ Not mentioned; appears to be cloud-based analytics |
| Cost per farmer | ❌ Not applicable—not sold as a per-farmer license to cooperatives |

**Verdict:** AtSource is relevant to your cooperative **only if** you sell through Olam's supply chain. If Olam is a buyer, they may deploy AtSource tools alongside your operations. Otherwise, this is not the right platform for an independent cooperative seeking its own management system.

### 2. FairTrace (FLOCERT)

**What it is:** FairTrace is an **online collaboration and assurance platform** operated by FLOCERT (the independent certifier for the Fairtrade system). It operates on a principle of "virtual handshakes"—one supply chain partner reports a transaction, and the other verifies it, creating mutual agreement on accuracy. It covers the supply chain from producers to end buyers. [[2]](https://www.flocert.net/app/uploads/2023/09/Chapter-6_Fairtrace.pdf) [[3]](https://www.flocert.net/fairtrace/)

**Critical limitation:** FairTrace is a **transaction verification and compliance tool for Fairtrade-certified entities**, not a field-level farm management or GPS mapping platform. It is accessed via a web portal requiring Fairtrade organization credentials.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Usability for basic phones | ❌ Web-based portal; no evidence of SMS/USSD interface for farmers |
| GPS plot mapping | ❌ Not a mapping tool |
| Certification integration | ✅✅ **Directly integrated with Fairtrade certification system**—this is its core purpose |
| Multi-language | ⚠️ Likely supports multiple languages given global Fairtrade operations, but not confirmed |
| Offline functionality | ❌ Appears to be online-only |
| Cost per farmer | ⚠️ Included as part of FLOCERT certification services for Fairtrade-certified organizations |

**Verdict:** FairTrace is **essential** if your cooperative pursues or maintains Fairtrade certification—but it is a compliance tool, not a farm management solution. It should be viewed as complementary to, not a replacement for, a field-level platform.

### 3. SourceTrace (DATAGREEN)

**What it is:** SourceTrace is a global SaaS platform providing digital agricultural value chain management software, operating in **37 countries**. Built around the DATAGREEN system, it offers farm management, supply chain logistics, and **blockchain-powered traceability**. It is specifically focused on smallholder farming and was awarded "Best in Digital Farming Technology – Traceability" at the ABSA 2021 summit. Clients include cooperatives, Cargill Cocoa & Chocolate, BRAC, and Chetna Organic. [[4]](https://sourcetrace.com)

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Usability for basic phones | ⚠️ Not explicitly documented; platform digitizes data "from remote farm level" but mechanism unclear |
| GPS plot mapping | ✅ Clients praise "efficiency of the mappings" (Goldtree); designed for farm-level mapping |
| Certification integration | ✅ Manages organic and agricultural certification standards; ISO 27001:2013 certified |
| Multi-language | ⚠️ Operating in 37 countries implies multi-language capability, but not explicitly detailed |
| Offline functionality | ⚠️ Claims to collect data "from remote farm level" but offline mode not explicitly confirmed |
| Cost per farmer | ❌ **Pricing not publicly available**—requires direct inquiry |

**Verdict:** SourceTrace is the **strongest candidate** among platforms researched for a cooperative seeking field-level management and traceability. Its smallholder focus, global footprint, and client base of cooperatives and NGOs suggest good fit. However, critical gaps remain: pricing, SMS/basic-phone support, and offline GPS accuracy in mountainous terrain must be confirmed through direct vendor engagement.

### 4. Farmforce

**What it is:** Farmforce is a SaaS platform focused on "food's first mile"—digital supply chain management for smallholder agriculture. It has been deployed for cocoa cooperatives in Côte d'Ivoire (covering ~6,000 smallholder farmers across 11 cooperatives for GCB Cocoa) and positions itself as a sustainability and compliance tool. [[5]](https://www.farmforce.com)

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Usability for basic phones | ⚠️ Cannot be confirmed from available data |
| GPS plot mapping | ⚠️ Likely supported given focus on field-level operations, but not confirmed |
| Certification integration | ✅ Explicitly built for compliance and sustainability programs |
| Multi-language | ⚠️ Not confirmed |
| Offline functionality | ⚠️ Not confirmed |
| Cost per farmer | ❌ Not publicly available |

**Verdict:** Farmforce is a plausible candidate given its smallholder focus and demonstrated deployment at similar scale (~6,000 farmers). However, significantly less information was available compared to SourceTrace. Requires direct vendor engagement for feature and pricing confirmation.

## Additional Platforms Worth Investigating

Given the search constraints, the following platforms are commonly cited in the agricultural traceability space and should be evaluated alongside the above:

- **CropIn** – AI-driven farm management platform operating in 90+ countries; strong data analytics but may be enterprise-priced
- **Agritask** – Crop and farm management with field monitoring; supports multiple crops including coffee
- **KoltiTrace** (by Koltiva) – Specifically designed for traceability and smallholder compliance; strong in SE Asia (relevant for Indonesia); supports GPS polygon mapping
- **Mabl (now part of Ecom)** – Coffee-specific traceability; limited to Ecom supply chains
- **Clarified (previously known as FarmLeaper)** – Emerging platform for smallholder supply chain management

## Recommendations for Your Cooperative

### 1. Adopt a layered approach, not a single platform
No single platform covers all needs. Consider:
- **Field-level management & traceability:** SourceTrace or Farmforce (request demos and pricing)
- **Fairtrade compliance:** FairTrace (mandatory if Fairtrade certified)
- **Rainforest Alliance compliance:** Check directly with RA about their new 2025 traceability requirements and which platforms they integrate with
- **Basic phone outreach:** A supplementary SMS/USSD service (e.g., via platforms like FarmerConnect or custom integration) for farmers without smartphones

### 2. Immediate action items
- **Contact SourceTrace and Farmforce** for demos tailored to your multi-country, multi-language (Spanish, Amharic, Bahasa Indonesia) deployment
- **Verify GPS polygon mapping accuracy** specifically in mountainous terrain (Cauca/Colombia highlands, Ethiopian highlands, Sumatran slopes) during demos
- **Request per-farmer pricing** for a 5,000-farmer deployment with field agent model (farmers don't need individual devices; field agents collect data)
- **Confirm offline sync capability** — critical for mountainous areas with intermittent connectivity
- **Engage Rainforest Alliance and Fairtrade** directly about recommended or pre-integrated technology partners

### 3. Cost management strategies
- **Field agent model** (1 agent per ~200 farmers = ~25 agents) rather than 5,000 individual farmer devices
- **Negotiate tiered SaaS pricing** based on farmer volume
- **Leverage certification premiums** — Fairtrade and Rainforest Alliance premiums can partially fund technology costs
- **Explore donor co-funding** — GIZ, USAID, and IDH frequently co-fund digitalization for cooperatives

## Uncertainty & Gaps

This research encountered significant data availability constraints. The following critical gaps remain **unresolved** and must be addressed through direct vendor engagement:

- **Pricing:** No publicly available per-farmer or per-cooperative pricing was found for any platform. This is the single most important gap for budget-constrained cooperatives.
- **SMS/USSD for basic phones:** None of the platforms clearly document basic phone interfaces. Many smallholder platforms use a "field agent with smartphone" model rather than requiring farmer devices—but this must be confirmed.
- **GPS accuracy in mountainous terrain:** No platform publicly documents GPS accuracy under challenging topographic conditions. GPS polygon mapping under forest canopy and steep terrain typically has 3–10 meter accuracy with consumer-grade devices, which may be sufficient for farm boundary delineation but should be validated.
- **Offline functionality details:** While platforms serving remote smallholders almost certainly have some offline capability, specifics on sync frequency, data caching, and offline mapping were not documented publicly.
- **Rainforest Alliance integration specifics:** The RA's 2020 Sustainable Agriculture Standard includes new traceability requirements, but which platforms have pre-built integration was not confirmed.
- **Multi-language support for target languages (Spanish, Amharic, Bahasa Indonesia):** SourceTrace's 37-country presence suggests capability, but language-specific localization was not confirmed for any platform.
- **AtSource availability:** It remains unclear whether AtSource can be licensed independently of an Olam supply relationship.
